# @requestport/theme

## 1.0.2

The palette moves into RequestPort, at the version the design system's
unreleased patch would have carried.

Taken from `cueplusplus/cue-ui` `packages/theme-requestport` at `ddb2c56a`,
including the AA corrections from cue-ui#117 that were never published: light
`fg-subtle` `#62696d` → `#5b6165`, light `accent` `#b8492b` → `#a14026` with
`focus` and `selection` following it, and dark `fg-subtle` `#81868a` → `#83888c`.

`theme.css` is byte-identical to the one cue-ui generates, apart from the banner
naming this package.
